USCN TRANSPARENCY WEBSITE - CORRECTED SURGICAL PUBLICATION EDITION
20 October 2026

WHAT WAS FIXED
The old line-based black-box redaction rule was incorrect: it hid entire
ordinary policy sentences if they contained generic words such as
"authentication", "security", "network", "incident" or "credentials".
This release discards those previously redacted PDF copies and restores
text from source DOCX documents available in the conversation.

DISCLOSURE AUDIT AND LIMITS
No actual credentials, access tokens, private technical endpoints or live
camera configuration values were identified in the available source policy
and template texts. Accordingly, NO black-bar text redactions were applied.
An absence of known secret-value patterns is not an independent security or
legal review; USCN should review the full text before publication.
No words like "the", "is" or "then" have been redacted.
Actual register case records are not reproduced; the public register document
is clearly an OVERVIEW, not a full register falsely blacked out line by line.

APPROVAL AND VERSIONS
USCN reports DPO Bertram Scanlon approved all DOCX documents on 20/10/2026.
Older source documents may contain historic earlier approval or draft labels.
POL-003 includes full historical source and approved current text, clearly
marked. Full original POL-006 v1.0 is not available; approved replacement
is reconstructed. Approval does not constitute publication or commissioning.
Individual DPIAs, technical checks and DPO operational permission remain
necessary, including camera-specific business-host live-viewing permissions.

WEBSITE AND DEPLOYMENT
ZIP contents (index.html, subfolders, robots.txt, sitemap.xml) must be
uploaded directly to the transparency.uscn.org.uk public document root.
The separate /camera/ builder must be retained and not overwritten.
The governance page has a direct supporting-documents link; main navigation
contains a Documents link. Homepage can be crawled once published publicly;
actual search-engine indexing is neither controlled nor guaranteed by a ZIP.
