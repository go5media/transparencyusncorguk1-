Host pilot arrangements

Restricted host live viewing

A limited, separately authorised live view for an eligible camera host—not a public stream or access to recorded footage.

A separate scheme from public live viewing

The public live-viewing programme concerns making selected assessed views available to members of the public. The restricted host live-viewing pilot is different: an eligible host may be authorised to see a defined live view from the camera installed at that host’s premises. Public-stream approval does not authorise host viewing, and host-viewing approval does not authorise publication to the public.

Business pilot first; residential expansion only after review

USCN may consider a limited pilot with selected eligible business hosts. Residential-host viewing is a potential later development, subject to assessment of the pilot, revised privacy documentation, relevant agreements and written camera-specific authorisation. No host is automatically entitled to join.

Restricted local access

Where expressly approved, access is read-only and limited to a named user, the authorised device, the camera’s approved view and an authenticated login. The proposed arrangement requires both valid individual account credentials (a username and password) and connection to the host’s specifically authorised local Wi-Fi network. The live-view service should not be accessible from an arbitrary remote network.

These are two separate access conditions, but Wi-Fi location alone is not necessarily a recognised independent multi-factor authentication factor. USCN must assess the actual technical protection, keep camera administration and stored footage inaccessible, and require additional authentication where the risk assessment calls for it.

Additional safeguards

Access may be time limited—for example, to a 60-second viewing window—with restrictions on repeated sessions. Other possible controls include privacy masking, constrained view, encrypted delivery, session logging, device restrictions and immediate revocation. The exact controls and whether host viewing is suitable must be documented for each camera before the DPO approves use.

What host viewing does not include

The pilot does not provide archived footage, playback, downloads, recordings export, camera movement, remote configuration or administrator access. A host wishing to report an incident or obtain footage uses the normal preservation and disclosure process.

Privacy and controller roles

The host must use any authorised live view only for its documented purpose and comply with viewer terms. If a host uses images for its own independent purposes, the host’s controller responsibilities and lawful basis may require separate assessment; USCN cannot promise complete immunity from data-protection duties.

Any proposal must be assessed under applicable UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 requirements. USCN can suspend or revoke viewing where privacy, security or operational needs justify it. Contact our data protection team with concerns.
