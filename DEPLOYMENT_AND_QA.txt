USCN TRANSPARENCY — FIXED STATIC WEBSITE

Deploy the CONTENTS of this ZIP directly into the document root for transparency.uscn.org.uk. index.html, robots.txt and sitemap.xml must be directly in that root. Do not upload an enclosing folder.

Directory pages: /policies/index.html, /documents/index.html and /cameras/index.html. The short /policies/, /documents/ and /cameras/ paths require DirectoryIndex index.html on the web server.

Keep your existing /camera/ builder separate. Search indexing requires the live website to return HTTP 200 and not be blocked or password protected; search engines decide whether to index. Submit /sitemap.xml after deployment.

Individual camera viewing remains conditional on assessment and authorisation. Read PUBLICATION_README.txt for document-control limitations.
