Public access and privacy review

Public live viewing

Potential public access to selected live streams, with camera-specific safeguarding and formal approval.

Purpose: access for members of the public

This page concerns a potential public live stream from selected USCN cameras. It is not the camera-host pilot and does not grant viewing rights to the host of a camera. Each proposed public stream must have a documented purpose and separate approval.

Not every camera has a public stream

Public viewing is considered only where an individual camera’s DPIA, live-view privacy assessment, technical safeguards and express DPO approval support it. Physical installation and approval for recording do not authorise public broadcasting.

Privacy and access

Where approved, viewing would ordinarily be free and could be open without an account. Case-specific restrictions may include viewing windows, registered accounts, passwords, privacy masking, reduced resolution or limited frame rate. The risks of exposing images of passers-by, private premises and vulnerable individuals must be assessed.

Public viewers receive no control of the camera

Any approved public stream is view-only and technically separated from camera administration and stored recordings. Public users cannot retrieve recordings, change the camera field of view or exercise remote camera controls.

Restricted host viewing is different

An eligible host may separately apply to a restricted business-host live-viewing pilot with named-user authorisation and local-access safeguards. The public viewing programme and host pilot have different audiences, purposes, approval decisions and controls. Approval of one does not mean approval of the other.

Availability and concerns

Viewing may be interrupted or suspended for privacy, security, compliance or technical reasons and is not guaranteed to remain continuously available. Concerns can be sent to data.protection@datahandling.uscn.org.uk.
