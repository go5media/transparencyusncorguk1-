Data protection

Your privacy rights

How to raise a concern or make a request about personal data captured by USCN CCTV.

What can I request?

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights of access, objection, erasure, restriction or rectification under UK data protection law. Rights are subject to statutory conditions and exemptions.

How to contact us

Email our data protection team: data.protection@datahandling.uscn.org.uk

Where possible, provide the camera ID or general area, approximate date and time, and the nature of your request. Do not send unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Response times

We respond within applicable statutory deadlines. Most subject access requests have a normal response period of one calendar month, subject to the relevant legal rules and exceptions.

Complaints

You may complain to USCN’s Data Protection Officer and, where appropriate, to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
